Advance Reader Invitation

Thank you for your interest in The Final Covenant.

For ten years, Gary Bednarczyk has survived alone in the rugged wilds of Riverside Park. When a violent street gang begins targeting women on the park's isolated trails, Gary steps out of the shadows—and into a dangerous confrontation with both the gang and a determined police detective.

The Final Covenant is a gritty vigilante thriller about a forgotten man forced to confront the violence society would rather ignore.

How to claim your Kindle copy

  1. If you've been invited to participate, look for the individual Amazon redemption link in the email you received from the author.
  2. Follow that link and complete the redemption process on Amazon. Sign in to the Amazon account you use for Kindle.
  3. Read the book at your own pace. There's no obligation to post a review.

Redemption links are provided individually to invited readers. They are not published on this page.

Your honest opinion matters

If you choose to leave a review, please share your genuine opinion—positive, negative, or somewhere in between. Reviews are entirely optional, and no particular rating or response is expected.
